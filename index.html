About Kedar

From Pokhara to Barcelona

Kedar Nath Dhungana is a Nepali entrepreneur based in Barcelona. Born in Pokhara and educated at Tribhuvan University, he now runs four businesses in the city: the Nepali supermarket Supermercat Industria, the Indian restaurant Atithi, and the gluten-free Japanese restaurants Aruku and Kobe.

Within the Nepali community he has served as President of Prabasi Nepali Manch Spain (Overseas Nepali Forum – Spain) and of Kaski Samaj Spain. On TikTok and Instagram he shares daily content about Nepali culture and the Nepali community in Spain.
